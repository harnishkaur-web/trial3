The Marking Card

One card, two sides. Side one is the 30-second routine you run on every AI answer. Side two is the short list of things that always need a second look — pinned above your workstation, usable in ten seconds flat.

Why this exists

An AI answer can sound completely certain and still be wrong about a date, a number or a name. This card is the habit that catches that — before the answer leaves your hands.

The card, both sides

Tap the card to flip it

Just like the printed version pinned above your workstation — one side is the routine, the other is the checklist.

Side 1 · The routine

30 seconds, every time

1
MarkUnderline every fact, figure, date, name and source in the answer.
2
SeparateSort each mark: matches a record, can't confirm, or contradicts a record.
3
CorrectVerify, replace, qualify or remove anything not clearly supported.
⏸ Pause before send: if you can't point to where a mark came from, don't send it yet.
Side 2 · The checklist

Always check these five

FactsClaims stated as true
FiguresNumbers, counts, percentages
DatesDays, months, years, durations
NamesPeople, places, organisations
SourcesWhere the claim says it came from

Side 2, in detail

Why each check matters

Facts
A claim stated as true needs a source before you trust it.
Figures
Numbers are easy to get slightly wrong and easy to check.
Dates
A vague date ("last Friday") can hide a wrong one.
Names
An AI tool may guess a name that sounds right but isn't.
Sources
"According to policy" means nothing if no policy is shown.
